TACTICAL BRIEFING

MISSION OBJECTIVE

Conquest Mode: Convert all enemy units to your fleet. Achieve total ideological dominance across 4 armies.

Survival Mode: Defend against endless waves of enemies. Survive as long as possible.

PEER PRESSURE DOCTRINE

Units surrounded by enemy forces will defect. When a boid is outnumbered by a single enemy team, conversion pressure builds until they switch allegiance.

💡 Isolate enemy units from their allies to convert them faster.

CONTROLS

Desktop:

  • LEFT CLICK - Scatter Pulse (repels ALL boids from cursor)
  • SPACE / ESC - Pause tactical view
  • R - Restart mission
  • ⚙️ Panel - Adjust flocking behavior in real-time

Mobile / Touch:

  • 💥 BUTTON - Scatter Pulse at touch position
  • DOUBLE TAP - Alternative scatter trigger
  • ⏸️ BUTTON - Pause / Resume game
  • DRAG - Move scatter target position

TACTICAL CONTROLS (In-Game Panel)

  • Separation - How strongly boids avoid crowding
  • Alignment - How much boids match neighbor velocity
  • Cohesion - How strongly boids group together
  • Max Speed - Movement speed of all units
  • Perception - How far boids can sense neighbors

POWERUPS

  • ⚡ Speed Boost - Temporarily increases fleet speed
  • 🛡️ Shield - Protects your fleet from conversion
  • 🧲 Magnet - Attracts nearby enemy units
  • 👥 Clone - Duplicates nearby friendly units

HAZARDS

Watch out for obstacles that appear on the battlefield:

  • Black Holes - Pull in and destroy nearby boids
  • Asteroids - Moving obstacles that destroy on contact

TACTICAL ADVANTAGE

Your units flock together naturally. Use the scatter pulse to herd enemies into traps, split enemy formations, and create local superiority. Encircle. Convert. Dominate.
